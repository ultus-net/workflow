## Summary

Build-first (TypeScript) reimplementation of the OpenShell network-rules algebra + advisor four-finding taxonomy and the NemoClaw SSRF denylist semantics — issue #438 (W178, NVIDIA adoption wave A1).

New pure module `src/integrations/egress-policy.ts` + `test/egress-policy.test.ts`. No IO/clock/SDK imports; kernel untouched.

- `decideEgress`: unordered rules, additive-allow union, deny-precedence, per-endpoint `audit` vs `enforce`.
- `validateEgressPolicy`: load-time rejection of rules sharing `host:port` that disagree on a safety field.
- `classifyDestination`/`isBlockedAddress`: loopback, link-local (incl. `169.254.169.254`), private, CGNAT, NAT64 `64:ff9b::/96`, 6to4 `2002::/16`, Teredo, IPv4-mapped, reserved names; every denylist entry carries an auditable `purpose` with a parity test.
- `assessPolicyChange`: `link_local_reach`, `l7_bypass_credentialed`, `credential_reach_expansion`, `capability_expansion` as pure set logic.

Closes #438.
